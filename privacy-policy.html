Privacy practices

Retro Street Games is provided by Ironleaf Studio. For privacy questions, email ironleafapps@gmail.com.

Information we use

  • If you sign in with Google, we use your account identifier and available profile information, such as display name and email address, to provide your account and online saves.
  • We store game progress such as scores, unlocked games, achievements, badges, daily challenges, and selected board themes. Signed-in progress can be stored in the cloud; progress from offline play is stored on your device. Android device backup may also copy local app data if backup is enabled on your device.
  • Public leaderboards show your player name, scores, and an account-specific identifier to other players. Avoid using a name containing information you want to keep private.

Service providers

We use Supabase Authentication for accounts and a Supabase database for online progress and leaderboards. Signing in with Google is optional; offline play does not require an account. See Supabase's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The app may show Google AdMob interstitial ads at breaks after games. An ad may appear when you choose to retry, play the next game, or return to the map. Debug builds use test ads.

Every ad request is configured for child-directed treatment and a maximum ad content rating of G. The app does not request Android advertising ID permissions or include another ad mediation network. Google disables interest-based and remarketing ads for requests marked as child-directed. Advertising services may still process information such as IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent abuse. See how Google uses information from partner apps.

Before requesting ads, the app checks consent status using Google's User Messaging Platform. Where a privacy choices form is required, you can open it from the account or About screen.

Intended audience

The app is intended for players age 13 or older. Offline play is available without an account. Players may choose to sign in with Google for online saves and public leaderboards.

Retention and security

We keep online account details and game progress while an account is active. Your device may retain offline progress until you clear the app's storage. We use Supabase database access controls and Android app storage to protect saved data. For a request to remove account data, use the contact method below. Service-provider backups and records required for security or legal reasons may remain for a limited period.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised.

Delete your account and data

To request deletion of your Retro Street Games account, email ironleafapps@gmail.com with the subject “Retro Street Games account deletion.” Include the email address used for your account so we can find it and verify ownership. Never send your password or sign-in codes.

Deletion requests cover the authentication account, saved game progress, achievements, badges, challenges, board themes, and public leaderboard entries associated with that account. Signed-in players can delete their account and online data in the app. You may also email us to request deletion; we verify ownership and handle email requests manually. We will tell you if any data must be retained for security or legal reasons.

Uninstalling the app does not delete online account data. To remove local data from a device, use Android Settings → Apps → Retro Street Games → Storage → Clear storage. This also removes offline progress on that device.
