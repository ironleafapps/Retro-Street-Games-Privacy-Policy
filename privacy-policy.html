Privacy policy

Retro Street Games is provided by Ironleaf Studio. Contact: [ADD SUPPORT EMAIL AND LEGAL OPERATOR DETAILS].

Information used by the app

  • When you sign in, the app uses your account identifier and available profile information, such as your display name and email address, to identify your account.
  • Game progress includes scores, unlocked games, achievements, badges, daily challenge progress, and selected board themes.
  • Signed-in progress can be stored online to restore it on another device. Offline progress is stored on your device, separately from signed-in progress.
  • Leaderboards display your player name and scores to other players. Do not use a name that reveals information you want to keep private.

Service providers

The app uses Google Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Cloud Firestore for online storage, and Firebase App Check to help protect online services. Google sign-in is optional; offline play does not require an account.

See Google's privacy policy for information about Google's practices.
